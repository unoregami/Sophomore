Database
	- an organized collection of information or data
		- it refers to information stored in a computer systems
		-- example:
			Libray (which is a database of books)
	- data
		- is raw and does not carry any specific meaning
	- information
		- is a group of organized data that contains logical meaning
Dewey Decimal System
	- it allows a librarian to organize the content of the library based on the division of all knowledge there is.

ERD 
	- Entity Relation Diagram
		- blueprint that visually shows the relationship of different pieces of information of different pieces of information that are connected
		- a diagram used to describe the structure of the database design. It aims to show what attributes (fields) and entity (collection) has. It show the relationship of attributes between entities.

	- one-to-many

	- many-to-many
